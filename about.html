About the Studio · Surat, Gujarat

We help local & emerging businesses build their true identity.
Because great products deserve more than just a box.

We are a fresh, dedicated team of creators starting our journey right here in Surat, Gujarat — a powerhouse of trade, textiles, craftsmanship, and entrepreneurial drive.

We saw firsthand that ambitious local businesses and new founders pour immense dedication into making outstanding products, only to wrap them in ordinary, off-the-shelf packaging that whispers in the crowd.

PackIt was born to change that. We don't just produce simple packaging; we partner with you to uncover and define your complete business identity — from core brand story and visual systems to calibrated structural dielines and tactile finishes.

Our mission is simple: empower homegrown and emerging brands with the identity and packaging presence to stand proudly beside the biggest names on any shelf.

Studio Principles

How we make decisions

01 / IDENTITY FIRST

Identity before the box

Packaging without an identity is just cardboard. We define your brand's unique reason for being, personality, and visual anchor before touching a dieline.

02 / HOMEGROWN PRIDE

Local roots, global benchmark

Rooted in Surat, Gujarat, we bring international-grade design rigor, typography, and finishing craft to local and emerging Indian businesses.

03 / TACTILE CRAFT

Engineered to be held

Packaging lives in hands and homes. We design for tactile weight, paper grain, functional closures, and unforgettable unboxing moments.
