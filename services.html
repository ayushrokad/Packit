01 / STRATEGY

Find the idea.

Before we design a box, we define why it matters. Great packaging is the distillation of a razor-sharp product truth.

What we deliver

Competitive Mapping

Analyzing the physical & digital shelf to pinpoint white space and design counter-programming.

Positioning & Differentiation

Defining the core proposition that gives consumers an immediate reason to pick your product over others.

Audience & Category Cues

Calibrating aesthetic codes so your product speaks natively to its intended buyers without being generic.

Product Narrative Architecture

Crafting claims hierarchy, regulatory disclosures, and unboxing storytelling sequence.

02 / IDENTITY

Make it recognizable.

A visual system engineered to perform at 3 inches on a supermarket shelf or in a 1-inch thumbnail on Shopify.

What we deliver

Logomark & Typographic Suite

Distinctive letterforms and icons crafted to emboss, stamp, and scale cleanly across substrates.

Colour Equity Systems

Proprietary palette engineering ensuring chromatic shelf-blocking and consistent pantone reproduction.

Packaging Grid Framework

Rules of hierarchy that keep secondary copy legible while preserving dramatic negative space.

Brand Guidelines

A rigorous, practical design manual for in-house teams, co-packers, and production facilities.

03 / PACKAGING

Make it stand out.

The physical craft of packaging. Custom shapes, tactile finishes, and sustainable structural engineering.

What we deliver

Structural & Form Design

Custom bottles, pouches, rigid cartons, and fold mechanisms engineered for ergonomics and production.

Material & Substrate Advisory

Selecting stocks, post-consumer recycled boards, bio-plastics, and foils that reflect quality and values.

Press-Ready Dielines

Production files pre-flighted with separation plates, varnishes, spot colors, and barcode verification.

Vendor & Co-packer Liaison

Direct coordination with domestic and global packaging printers to guarantee intent matches reality.

Unboxing Choreography

Retail Point-of-Sale (POS)

Digital E-Commerce Integration

Merchandising & Secondary Goods
